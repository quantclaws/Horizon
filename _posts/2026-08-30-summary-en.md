## [Tencent releases open‑source Hy4 preview with recursive self‑improvement.](https://www.tencent.com/tencent-releases-and-open-sources-tencent-hy4-preview/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tencent has released an open‑source preview of its Hy4 AI model, a 770B‑parameter Mixture‑of‑Experts model with 49B active parameters and a context window exceeding 1 million tokens, featuring early recursive self‑improvement capabilities. The release signals a step toward self‑optimizing large language models and shows strong early traction on OpenRouter, where it processed trillions of tokens at a low 5% cache cost, highlighting potential gains in efficiency and accessibility. Hy4 preview uses a Mixture‑of‑Experts architecture, activating 49B parameters per token, supports >1M‑token contexts, and has already participated in automating its own training‑method and data‑strategy experiments as part of a recursive self‑improvement loop.

Recursive self‑improvement in language models refers to a model’s ability to propose, test, and adopt changes to its own training or architecture, creating a feedback loop that can enhance performance. OpenRouter is a unified API platform that aggregates multiple LLM providers, allowing developers to route requests to the most suitable model with minimal latency.

## [NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope set for Falcon Heavy launch.](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/roman-space-telescope/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope is scheduled to launch on August 30, 2026 aboard a Falcon Heavy rocket, delivering wide-field imaging and fully open data at a rate of up to 1.4 TB per day. Its mission will study dark energy, supernovae, and exoplanets. With a field of view at least 100 times larger than Hubble’s, Roman can survey vast swaths of the sky in a fraction of the time, enabling unprecedented statistical studies of cosmic structure. Its open‑data policy will allow scientists worldwide to download and analyze observations immediately, accelerating discoveries in dark energy, supernova cosmology, and exoplanet detection. The Wide‑Field Instrument is a 300.8‑megapixel visible‑near‑infrared camera covering a 0.28 sq deg field, while the Coronagraph Instrument will test advanced starlight‑suppression techniques for direct exoplanet imaging. The spacecraft will downlink as much as 1.4 TB of raw compressed data daily, with all data released publicly without any proprietary period.

hackernews · JumpCrisscross · Aug 29, 15:48 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49490870)

**Background**: The Roman Space Telescope reuses a 2.4‑meter primary mirror originally built for a National Reconnaissance Office spy satellite, donated to NASA. Named after NASA’s first chief astronomer, Nancy Grace Roman, the mission was ranked the top priority in the 2010 Astrophysics Decadal Survey and completed construction on 25 November 2025. It is destined for a Sun–Earth L2 orbit, where it will conduct its wide‑field and coronagraph surveys.

The Department of Homeland Security has been using 19 U.S.C. § 1509, an obscure customs provision, to obtain months of phone and text records from journalists, non-profits, and unions without prior notice or judicial oversight. This practice raises serious concerns about government overreach, threatens press freedom and privacy, and could set a precedent for broader surveillance of civil society groups under the guise of customs enforcement. In one case, DHS obtained six months of phone records for journalist Georgia Fort, encompassing over 10,000 calls and texts, and served Google with a summons for YouTube data, while the target was not notified until mid‑July when the records were produced to her lawyers.

Companies often comply with the summons without challenge, allowing the agency to obtain data without prior judicial approval.

## [Tencent releases Hy4 Preview, a 770B‑parameter open‑weight LLM](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/29/hy4/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tencent announced Hy4 Preview, an open‑weight text‑only LLM with 770 B total parameters, 49 B active parameters, and a 1 M‑token context window, surpassing its Hy3 predecessor. The model’s massive scale and unprecedented context length push the frontier of open‑weight LLMs, enabling longer‑form reasoning and broader research use, and signal Tencent’s growing investment in large‑scale AI. Hy4 Preview employs a mixture‑of‑experts architecture, defaults to a 'high' reasoning effort level with an optional 'no_think' mode, and its chat template enforces these two settings via Jinja conditionals.

rss · Simon Willison · Aug 29, 23:53

**Background**: Open‑weight LLMs release their model parameters publicly, allowing researchers to inspect and fine‑tune them. Parameter counts distinguish total size from the subset of active parameters used during inference, which affects compute cost. The context window determines how many tokens the model can attend to at once, influencing its ability to handle long documents. Mixture‑of‑experts models route tokens to specialized sub‑networks, improving efficiency at massive scales. Reasoning effort parameters let users trade off response speed against depth of internal chain‑of‑thought processing, while chat templates define how conversation histories are formatted for the model.
