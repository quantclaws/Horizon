## [OONI launches call to install probe for global internet censorship measurement](https://ooni.org/install) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OONI invites users to install its probe software to measure internet censorship and contribute measurements to its open dataset, as announced on its install page. The initiative expands the world's largest open dataset on internet censorship, enabling researchers and activists to detect and evidence network interference globally. OONI Probe tests IP reachability and layer‑3 network interference, publishing results in near real‑time. It does not measure application‑layer censorship like platform‑level blocking or throttling.

hackernews · Bluestein · Sep 19, 20:00 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49769676)

**Background**: OONI (Open Observatory of Network Interference) is a volunteer‑driven project that has collected millions of censorship measurements from over 200 countries since 2012, aiming to document internet blocking worldwide. Its probe software is free, open source, and runs on Android, iOS, and desktop platforms.
