## [Asahi Linux Announces Official Support for Apple M3 Chip](https://asahilinux.org/2026/09/m2-episode-1/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Asahi Linux has released official support for Apple's M3 chip, allowing Linux to run on M3‑based Macs. This achievement extends Linux compatibility to Apple’s latest silicon, encouraging broader adoption and development on Mac hardware while showcasing the progress of reverse‑engineered driver efforts. The M3 chip is built on TSMC’s 3‑nanometer process and includes up to a 10‑core GPU with Dynamic Caching, hardware‑accelerated ray tracing and mesh shading. Asahi Linux now offers mainline kernel drivers for the GPU, USB4 and Thunderbolt support across M1, M2 and M3 SoCs.

hackernews · mdp2021 · Sep 6, 14:08 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586698)

**Background**: Asahi Linux is a community‑driven project that reverse‑engineers Apple Silicon SoCs to port the Linux kernel and related software to Macs. The Apple M3, introduced in October 2023, is Apple’s first personal‑computer chip built on TSMC’s 3‑nm process, featuring an 8‑core CPU and up to a 10‑core GPU with new graphics technologies. While the Linux kernel already includes ARM64 support, full hardware enablement on Apple Silicon has relied on Asahi Linux’s drivers for GPU, USB4, Thunderbolt and power management.

The flight demonstrates Europe's growing private launch capability, reduces dependence on Arianespace, and moves the continent toward sovereign, responsive access to space for commercial and governmental customers. Spectrum is a two‑stage, liquid‑fueled rocket developed largely in‑house by Isar Aerospace, capable of delivering up to 1,000 kg to low Earth orbit; the launch used the company's Aquila engines and took place from the Andøya spaceport.

**Background**: Europe has long pursued independent access to space through the Ariane family of launchers, but recent delays and cost growth with Ariane 6 have spurred interest in private alternatives. Founded in 2018 near Munich, Isar Aerospace aims to provide flexible, responsive launch services with its in‑house developed Spectrum rocket and Aquila engines.
